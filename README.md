**Save and switch between your Minecraft configurations instantly!**

Tired of manually tweaking your settings every time you switch between recording, streaming, or casual play? Config Presets lets you save complete snapshots of your game settings and apply them with a single click.

## 📦 What Can You Save?
- **Video Settings** - Graphics quality, render distance, FOV, particles, and more
- **Sound Settings** - All volume levels and audio preferences
- **Controls & Keybinds** - Your complete control scheme
- **Resource Packs** - Enabled packs with load order preserved
- **Accessibility & Chat** - Language, narrator, chat settings, and more
- **Mod Configurations** - Third-party mod settings
## 🎯 Quick Access
- **Keybind** - Press P (customizable) to open the preset manager from anywhere
- **Menu Buttons** - Quick-access buttons on the title and pause screens
## 🏷️ Organization
- **Folders** - Organize presets into categories
- **Tags** - Add colored tags for easy filtering
- **Search** - Find presets by name or tag instantly
## 💾 Import/Export
- **Share Presets** - Export as JSON files to share with friends
- **Mod Lists** - Optionally include a list of your installed mods
## 🚀 How to Use
- Install the mod and launch Minecraft
- Press P or click the wrench icon in the pause menu
- Click "Create New" and configure what to save:
- Toggle which categories to include (Video, Sound, Controls, etc.)
- Name your preset and optionally add tags/folders
- Click "Apply" on any preset to switch instantly!
## 🎨 Perfect For...
- **Content Creators** - Separate presets for recording vs. streaming
- **Modpack Players** - Save different configs for different packs
- **Performance Tuning** - Quickly switch between quality and performance
- **Multiplayer** - Different settings for servers vs. singleplayer
- **Testing** - Rapidly test different configurations

- [Fabric Loader 0.19.2+](https://fabricmc.net/)

- [Cloth Config API](https://modrinth.com/mod/cloth-config)
- [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) (optional, for easy access to config)

NeoForge:
- [NeoForge Loader](https://neoforged.net)
- [Cloth Config API](https://modrinth.com/mod/cloth-config)
- [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) (optional, for easy access to config)
## 🔧 Compatibility
